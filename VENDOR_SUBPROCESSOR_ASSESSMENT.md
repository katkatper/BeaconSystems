# Vendor and subprocessor assessment

Owner: Vendor Risk; approvals: Security, Privacy/Legal and Service Owner.
No vendor receives Restricted data or production credentials before approval.

The inventory records purpose, data/classes, locations/subprocessors, BAA/DPA/CJIS
terms, security evidence, owner/renewal and decision for hosting, communication,
monitoring, support and every other production dependency.

Review tenant isolation, encryption/key ownership, access/personnel controls,
independent tests, incident duties, availability/DR, deletion/return, vulnerabilities,
subprocessor changes, residency/transfers, government requests, audit rights,
insurance and termination assistance. Critical vendors are reassessed annually and
on material change/incident. Exceptions require an owner, compensating control,
expiry and remediation.
