# Phase VI compliance and product readiness

Phase VI turns Beacon's technical controls into reviewable governance evidence
and validates the investigator experience. These artifacts support assessment;
they do not constitute HIPAA, CJIS, privacy or legal certification.

## Repository scope

- [x] Security risk assessment and treatment register.
- [x] Data classification and retention policy.
- [x] Incident-response and tabletop package.
- [x] Access-review and termination procedure.
- [x] BAA applicability and counsel-review checklist.
- [x] CJIS Security Policy v6.1 family mapping.
- [x] Personnel screening and awareness requirements.
- [x] Encryption/key-management evidence register.
- [x] Vendor/subprocessor assessment.
- [x] Nationwide/international privacy launch gate.
- [x] Automated Chromium/WebKit browser and accessibility matrix completed.
- [ ] Role-based UAT completed by designated users.
- [ ] Security, privacy/legal and agency approvals obtained.

Unchecked items may not be represented as complete in licensing, contracting or
production-authorization materials. Evidence identifies owner, date, environment,
reviewer and corrective actions.
