# Business associate agreement review

This is a counsel-review aid, not legal advice or an executed agreement.

For each customer/vendor, counsel records whether it is a covered entity, business
associate, subcontractor or outside HIPAA; what ePHI Beacon creates, receives,
maintains or transmits; and the rationale. When ePHI is handled for a regulated
entity, the required written agreement must be effective before processing.

Counsel validates: parties/services; permitted/required PHI use; prohibited use;
Security Rule safeguards; incident/breach duties and timelines; subcontractor
flow-down; support for access/amendment/accounting and HHS inspection; return or
destruction at termination; cure/termination rights; survival/retention; location,
audit rights, insurance, indemnity and governing law.

Maintain a register containing relationship, role determination, ePHI scope,
agreement/effective date, counsel, renewal date and subprocessor coverage. Initial
entries must cover hosting, communications, monitoring and support vendors.
