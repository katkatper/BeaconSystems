# Nationwide and international privacy/legal launch review

Owner: Privacy Counsel. This checklist is not legal advice.

For every jurisdiction: identify legal roles; purposes/authority/lawful basis;
minimization; CJI, health, biometric/genetic, minor, victim and location data;
notice/consent/rights/public-records duties; retention/hold/deletion; automated
matching transparency and human review; breach notices; residency/exports;
agreements (DPA, BAA, CJIS); vendors; insurance; accessibility and language duties.

International launch remains disabled as a business decision until counsel records
the country, transfer mechanism, localization/hosting, representative/DPO duties,
law-enforcement restrictions, individual rights and conflict-of-law analysis. For
EEA/UK data, record roles, lawful basis, DPIA need, adequacy/SCC or alternative and
supplementary safeguards. U.S. approval never authorizes another country.

The launch register records jurisdiction/use, counsel, assessment, contracts,
technical restrictions and signed decision/date. Every entry starts Blocked.
