# Privacy, records and data-subject request runbook

Owner: Privacy Officer; Legal and agency Records Officer determine applicability.

1. Authenticate requester and authority without collecting excessive identity data.
2. Classify request: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability,
   accounting, objection, public record, subpoena/court order or agency request.
3. Identify jurisdictions, tenants, cases, legal holds, CJI/ePHI and victim/minor risks.
4. Preserve deadlines and acknowledge using approved language.
5. Search only authorized systems; log scope, query, reviewer and exports.
6. Apply exemptions, redactions, holds and third-party consultation through counsel.
7. Use two-person approval before disclosure, correction or disposition.
8. Deliver through an approved encrypted channel and verify receipt.
9. Record decision, authority, timestamps, evidence and appeal/complaint instructions.

Beacon does not automatically grant deletion. Case, evidence, CJI, health, public-
records and litigation requirements may prohibit it. Backups expire through the
approved schedule rather than being selectively rewritten unless counsel requires it.
