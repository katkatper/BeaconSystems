# Personnel screening and security awareness

Owner: HR/People Operations with Security and the agency CJIS authority.

Before access, verify identity, role, agency sponsorship, required fingerprint or
criminal-history screening, lawful sanctions checks, agreements and training. The
authorized CJIS authority/jurisdiction defines screening requirements.

Training occurs before access, annually and after material changes. It covers
CJI/PII/ePHI, phishing, MFA/passwords, remote/mobile work, least privilege, social
engineering, incident reporting, media, physical security, exports, records requests
and sanctions. Evidence includes learner, curriculum/version, completion time,
assessment, acknowledgement, exceptions and retraining. Required access is blocked
or suspended when training/screening expires; unnecessary screening detail is not
stored in Beacon.
